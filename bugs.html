Before you report

  • Some things that look like bugs are on purpose: the market never sells ore or bars, and visitors only arrive on their timer. The FAQ explains these.
  • If a purchase didn't arrive, close the game completely and open it again first.
  • We read every report, but can't always reply to each one.
