Blacksmith Legends · October 2026

On 4 October 2026 the game took its own name, Blacksmith Legends, and these changes followed.

18 October

  • Mine notifications. A new Mine switch in Settings tells you when your crates are full, and names which ones: "Full crates in the mine: Copper Ore, Tin Ore". It updates itself as more fill, rather than piling up.
  • All new help. Every help page and tip is rewritten for the game as it is now, in all ten languages, with a new page for the mine. The help index lists its topics in your own language, in the order you meet them.
  • Better translations. Around 250 buttons, tabs, settings and upgrade names are retranslated in every language.
  • The assistant notification finally says something, and no notification goes off while you're playing.
  • Premium follows your Google Play account. The game checks with Google Play when it starts: premium bought on your account comes back on a new phone automatically, and premium that was refunded is removed. If Google Play can't be reached, nothing changes, so playing offline never costs you premium.
  • Coal Mining Speed stacks on top of everything else. It used to be added to Mining Speed, so every Mining Speed step watered it down. Now it multiplies, so coal keeps up with the bars that burn it however fast the rest of your mine digs.
  • The Statistics screen shows how fast each open floor is digging, per minute or per hour.
  • The trade screen lists what a visitor pays most for first, with a line under the three best offers.
  • Coin packs are rebalanced: 1,000 / 3,000 / 7,500 coins per level, with each prestige adding half as much again. The bigger packs are better value, but not wildly so.
  • The Contribute purchase is gone.
  • The buy-coins + in the top bar always shows. It used to vanish for some premium players who had once turned adverts off.
  • Settings are laid out as a simple list. The old slow-phone switches (Auto Refresh, Full Screen Check and Update Slots) are gone, since turning them off only broke things.
  • Messages give counts as plain numbers ("Hauled 40 Iron Ore", not "40x"), and the assistant screen shows what an assistant gives you now before what each level adds.
  • In-game messages are retranslated in Japanese, Chinese and Korean.
  • Successful adventures read as wins out of attempts: 4 / 15, not 15 / 4.
  • No more crackle in the music when moving between rooms, and no Android click on every tap when the phone's touch sounds are on.
  • Queueing a very large batch no longer stutters, and a slot's queue count stays short.
  • Upgrade rows are separated, with each name in gold.

17 October

  • A Help & FAQ button in Settings opens this website's FAQ.
  • Gems in the mine. Any ore you dig can contain a gem in its floor's own colour: ruby in copper and dragonite, citrine in gold, onyx in coal and iron, and so on.
  • Gem Finding, a new upgrade with no end that raises the gem chance. The upgrade screen shows the real chance, not just the bonus.
  • Endless upgrades now buy one step per tap, with no confirmation each time; the row says exactly what each step costs.
  • Mine achievements: one for reaching each floor from coal down to dragonite, three for finding gems, one for the Mine Lift and one for +50% Mining Speed.
  • New leaderboards: Ores Mined, Gems Found, Mining Speed and Coins Earned, each with its own button on the Statistics screen.
  • Visitors arrive twice as often, and paying to bring one in early has gone. A visitor no longer walks in the instant the last one leaves.
  • Mr T Utorial stays out of the random visitors, and Father Christmas only visits in December.
  • Every gem stall sells all seven gems, and turns up often enough to find.
  • Restock All is priced as a share of the stock it brings back, scaled to your level.
  • Selling shows the coins still to come, amounts read as 15k / 1.2M, and prices have their digits grouped.
  • Inventory and trade prices are drawn as proper buttons, centred even when a long item name wraps; books and pages, which can't be sold, have none.
  • Save export and import are gone, so every leaderboard score is earned. Cloud saves through Google Play Games still move your game between phones.

11 October

  • Swiping fast through the mine shows each room's picture straight away instead of grey.
  • Crafting and visitor screens do less work behind the scenes, so long games stay quick.

6 October

  • Every market stall has its own shopkeeper, and no two share a face.
  • A trader's stock grows the more you buy from them, up to fifty times its start.
  • Upgrades are listed by area (mine, workshop, visitors, market, bonuses) instead of A to Z.
  • The lantern beside the bonus chest is the bonus light, and it goes fully dark while the chest is counting down.
  • Selling and huge Max crafts no longer freeze the screen.
  • A Max button for crushing gems at the gem table.
  • Crafting slots lose an empty black band, and very large overflow counts stop wrapping.

5 October

  • Redrawn late-game items, item type icons and the quests icon.

4 October

  • The game is renamed Blacksmith Legends, with a new logo, icon and store artwork.
  • Coal Mining Speed, paid in coins and black-enchanted pickaxes.
  • Mining Speed and Coal Mining Speed carry on past their tenth step, for players who want ore pouring up the shaft.
  • Redrawn pickaxes, bronze to dragon.
  • No adverts at all. The "watch an advert" options are gone: the bonus chest simply opens when tapped.
  • Every flat tan background is now stone.
  • Two new super upgrades: 2x Mined Ore and 2x Visitor Gifts.
  • A crafting slot that opens partway through a queue takes waiting crafts straight away.
  • Locked traders no longer restock for free.
  • A Max toggle on the gem table, and assorted trade and visitor screen improvements.

Idle Mine and Smithing · September 2026

For its first five weeks the game went by the working name Idle Mine and Smithing, and most of what makes it its own game arrived in this time.

A new world

  • The single scrolling scene became hand-painted rooms you move between: an entrance hall with its market stall and hiring board, a workshop, a storeroom, and the mine below.
  • New dark stone panels on every screen, a forged experience bar, and light, readable text on every button.
  • Fires and lanterns that glow, and a forge that burns only while it's working.
  • Redrawn items, visitors, characters, icons and the Legendary set.
  • Music in the lobby that muffles as you go deeper, coins you can hear, and dripping water and picks in the mine.
  • What a leaving visitor gives you is laid on the lobby desk.

The mine

  • A ten-floor mine, one ore per floor from copper to dragonite, opening as you level up. Each crate fills in about fifty minutes.
  • Ore and bars are no longer sold at the market: ore is mined, and bars are smelted.
  • The Mine Lift hauls ore up for six hours after you last played, and its crane appears in the storeroom once bought.
  • Mining Speed, paid in coins and pickaxes, with enchanted pickaxes speeding up the ores that match their gem.

Trading and visitors

  • Buying at the market is instant, and each of its slots is a stall with a trader.
  • Buy All for each trader, and one that really does clear the market.
  • The market buys and sells at the same price.
  • Every visitor's preferences and every trader's and visitor's name are shuffled for each new game, with a spoken clue to one preference.
  • The sell button shows what a visitor will really pay.

Crafting

  • Pick what to craft from a grid of everything, instead of swiping one item at a time.
  • Crafting slots fill up smoothly instead of counting down in whole seconds.
  • Cancel Queue refunds every ingredient.
  • Items show their state as a badge on the picture, and items above your level are greyed out with their real names instead of hidden.
  • Up to ten super upgrades at once.
  • The assistants are named after their art and their progress bar says what it measures.

Fixes and speed

  • A new, faster save database.
  • Many fixes: crafts paying out exactly once, the assistant's boost no longer growing on its own, 2x Coin Earnings and 2x Trader Stock working as described, and toasts no longer swallowing taps meant for buttons.
  • The game is portrait-only, and keeps clear of rounded screen corners.

Pixel Blacksmith · 2015 to 2026

Everything above is built on Pixel Blacksmith by Jake Lee.

  • 2015 to 2018: Jake builds and grows the game, over more than a thousand changes: smelting, forging and enchanting, hundreds of items and recipes, visitors with preferences and trophies, traders, upgrades, heroes, helpers, assistants, prestige, and translations by its players.
  • 2018 to 2022: further updates.
  • 2024 (3.0): rebuilt for modern Android.
  • 2026 (3.1): brought up to date with current Android, Google Play Billing and Google Play Games, with cloud saves restored.
  • Jake released the whole game as open source under the MIT licence, which is where Blacksmith Legends began.
