Where it began: Pixel Blacksmith, by Jake Lee

Blacksmith Legends is built on Pixel Blacksmith, a crafting game made by Jake Lee. He spent years building it, then generously released the whole game as open source under the MIT licence, which is the only reason this game exists.

The crafting system, the hundreds of items and recipes, the visitors, the heroes and helpers, the translations and a great deal of the code are his work. Blacksmith Legends started from all of it, and it's still the foundation everything here stands on.

Thank you, Jake.

  • Pixel Blacksmith on GitHub: github.com/JakeSteam/PixelBlacksmith
  • Jake's other projects: github.com/JakeSteam

Pixel Blacksmith is © 2022 Jake Lee and is used under the MIT licence. Blacksmith Legends is an independent project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Jake.

What's changed

Blacksmith Legends has since grown into its own game. The biggest differences:

A mine of your own

  • A ten-floor mine below the shop, opening floor by floor as you level up. Ore is dug over time rather than bought, and bars come only from smelting it.
  • Gems found in mined ore, each floor in its own colour.
  • New upgrades: the Mine Lift, Mining Speed, Coal Mining Speed and Gem Finding, the speed and gem ones without an end.

A new look and sound

  • Hand-painted rooms you move between, from the entrance hall and its market stall down through the workshop and storeroom into the mine, replacing the original single scrolling scene.
  • Stone panels, a new logo and icon, animated forge and lantern light.
  • Lobby music that fades and muffles as you go deeper, and its own sound in the mine.

A different economy

  • The market buys and sells at the same price, buying is instant, and every stall has its own shopkeeper whose stock grows the more you trade.
  • Visitors' tastes and names are shuffled for every new game.
  • Cancelling a crafting queue refunds everything.
  • No adverts at all.

Under the bonnet

  • Updated for current Android versions, with a new save database, the current Google Play Games and Google Play Billing, and many speed and stability fixes.
  • New achievements and leaderboards for the mine.
