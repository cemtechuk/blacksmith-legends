Delete your Blacksmith Legends data

Blacksmith Legends, by Cemtech, has no accounts of its own. Your data is in at most three places, and you can delete each of them yourself.

1. Your game progress on your phone

Your save and settings are stored only on your phone. To delete them, uninstall the game, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → Blacksmith Legends → Storage → Clear storage. They are deleted immediately and nothing is kept.

2. Your Google Play Games data

If you signed in to Google Play Games, your achievements, leaderboard scores and any cloud save for this game are stored by Google under your Google account. To delete them:

  1. Open your Play Games profile (or the Play Games settings on your phone).
  2. Choose Delete Play Games account & data, then under individual game data find Blacksmith Legends and delete it. Deleting your whole Play Games account removes the data for every game.

Google deletes this data and keeps it only as described in the Google Privacy Policy. We keep no copy of it.

3. Emails you sent us

If you emailed support or sent a bug report, ask us to delete it by emailing blacksmithlegendssupport@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data”, from the address you wrote from. We delete those messages and your address within 30 days and keep nothing.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play, which keeps your purchase history under your Google account as described in the Google Privacy Policy. The game itself stores only which items you own, on your phone, and that goes with step 1.
