The game

Is the game free?

Yes. It's free to download and play, with no adverts. You can optionally buy Premium or coin packs, but everything in the game can be reached without paying.

Does the game keep going while it's closed?

Yes. Each mine floor's crate keeps filling until it's full (about fifty minutes), and your crafting queues keep working. If you've bought the Mine Lift, it keeps hauling ore up for six hours after you last had the game open.

Where do I get ore and bars? The market doesn't sell them.

That's on purpose: ore comes from the mine, and bars come from smelting it in the furnace. Head down past the workshop to the storeroom and take the hatch into the mine, then tap a floor's crate to collect what's been dug.

How do I open more mine floors?

By levelling up. Copper and tin are open from the start; then coal opens at level 5, iron at 8, mithril at 20, adamantite at 30, silver at 35, gold at 38, runite at 40 and dragonite at 50.

How do I find gems?

Every ore you dig has a small chance of containing a gem, in the floor's own colour. The Gem Finding upgrade raises the chance.

Why does a visitor want something I've never heard them mention?

Every visitor has their own preferences, and they're shuffled for every new game, so nobody's tastes are the same twice. Each visitor gives you a clue; the rest you learn by trading with them.

What is prestige?

Premium players who reach level 70 can start a New Game Plus. Items, coins, upgrades and level reset, but you keep your statistics and earn +50% coins from then on (with -25% XP).

Premium and purchases

What does Premium give me?
  • +20% to the bonus coins and bonus XP upgrades straight away, and their maximum raised from 50% to 100%.
  • An extra crafting slot in every area.
  • Seventeen legendary items, and easier access to Super Upgrades.
  • Doubled bonus chest rewards, more often.
  • The ability to prestige at level 70.
I bought something but didn't get it.

Close the game completely and open it again: purchases that were interrupted are delivered on the next launch. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.).

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Google Play. You can request one from your Google Play order history.

Saves, devices and Google Play Games

How do I move my game to a new phone?

Use a Google Play Games cloud save. On your old phone, open Settings in the game and save to the cloud under Google Play. On the new phone, sign in to the same Google account and load that save.

Where are achievements and leaderboards?

In Settings, under Google Play, and on the Statistics screen next to each statistic that has a leaderboard. You need to be signed in to Google Play Games.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 6.0 or newer. It's designed for phones, in portrait.

Which languages are there?

English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Dutch, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese. You can switch language in Settings.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Blacksmith Legends is Android only for now.

Problems

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Email blacksmithlegendssupport@gmail.com with what you were doing, what happened, and your phone model. The game version is at the bottom of the Statistics screen.
