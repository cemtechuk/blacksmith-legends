Dig, smelt, forge, sell

Run a blacksmith's shop above a mine. Dig ore floor by floor, smelt it into bars, forge tools and armour, and trade with the townsfolk who come through your door, each with their own tastes.

Coming soon to Google Play.

What's in the game

  • A ten-floor mine, from copper and tin at the top to dragonite at the bottom. Each floor's crate fills while you're away, and the deeper you go the rarer the ore.
  • Gems in the ore: every floor can turn up a gem in its own colour.
  • Smelt, forge and enchant hundreds of items, from bronze daggers to dragon armour.
  • Visitors with their own tastes: every new game rolls who likes what, and it's up to you to work them out.
  • A market of stalls that grow their stock the more you trade with them.
  • Upgrades that never stop: Mining Speed, the Mine Lift, Gem Finding and more.
  • Achievements and leaderboards through Google Play Games.
  • No adverts. Ever.
