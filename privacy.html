Privacy Policy

Effective 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Blacksmith Legends game for Android (“the game”). In short: the game has no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, it does not sell or share your data, and your progress is stored on your own device.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved on your phone, inside the game's own storage. They never leave the device unless you use Google Play Games cloud saves, described below. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for achievements, leaderboards and cloud saves. Your achievements, leaderboard scores and, if you choose to save to the cloud, a copy of your game save are stored by Google under your Google account. Leaderboards show your Play Games profile name and avatar to other players, according to your Play Games privacy settings. The game does not receive your email address or any other account details. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage or delete it in the Play Games app or your Google account settings.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The game is told only which item was bought so it can deliver it. It never sees your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game shows reminders (for example, when a visitor arrives or a craft finishes). They are created on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Emailing support

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we do not share them.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
